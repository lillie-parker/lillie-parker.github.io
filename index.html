Campus Tours, University of Oregon

Designed and developed as a GIS Analyst at the University of Oregon. The self-guided tours offer a way to engage with the campus from anywhere in the world. Prospective or current students and their families, faculty, staff, and community members all have the opportunity to explore the campus culture, history, and sustainability efforts. Once a trifold brochure, each iteration of the web application has expanded on the information provided. I have stewarded the tours applications since 2020, spearheading accessibility improvements with the inclusion of audio files with captions and collaborating with partners to offer a Spanish translation of the Main Campus Tour. My involvement included:

  • Facilitating updates; working with key partners maintain accuracy and modernize the self guided tour
  • Managing data sources and writing web APIs (C#)
  • Developing each application with custom UX features (TypeScript, HTML, CSS)
  • View Project

Campus Addresses, University of Oregon

Designed and developed as a GIS Analyst at the University of Oregon. This project was designed to simplify the process of finding campus building addresses, primarily for employees. My involvement included:
